Stories worth remembering

Cabo Verde made their debut count

They drew all three group matches against Spain, Uruguay, and Saudi Arabia. They then took Argentina to extra time before losing 3–2. It was a remarkable first World Cup.

Paraguay knocked out Germany

After a 1–1 draw, Orlando Gill saved penalties from Kai Havertz and Nick Woltemade as Paraguay won the shootout 4–3.

Curaçao earned their first World Cup point

Eloy Room kept the clean sheet, while captain Leandro Bacuna and Tahith Chong helped Curaçao hold Ecuador to a 0–0 draw and earn their first World Cup point.
