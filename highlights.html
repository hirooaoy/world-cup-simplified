A few more highlights

The stories that kept coming up after the final whistle.

Cabo Verde made their debut count.

They drew all three group matches — against Spain, Uruguay, and Saudi Arabia — then took Argentina to extra time before losing 3–2. A remarkable first World Cup.

Paraguay knocked out Germany.

After a 1–1 draw, Orlando Gill saved penalties from Kai Havertz and Nick Woltemade as Paraguay won the shootout 4–3.

Morocco went deep again.

After their historic semi-final run in 2022, Morocco reached the quarter-finals again — beating the Netherlands on penalties and Canada 3–0.

Also worth remembering: Yoane Wissa scored DR Congo’s first-ever World Cup goal, while debutants Curaçao scored against Germany and held Ecuador to a 0–0 draw.
