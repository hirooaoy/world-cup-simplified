Sixteen cards changed and four stayed exactly as they were. The blind comparison contained 17 proposed revisions: 13 were preferred by both reviewers, three were preferred by one reviewer and tied by the other, and one was tied by both reviewers and rejected. Before:

> The first detail to track with Messi is carrying into open grass before the block can reset. Watch how he carries with his head up to see the second defender. In a separate phase, he moves toward the far post as the attack develops on the opposite side.

Final:

Evidence: FIFA's accounts distinguish the young substitute from Riquelme's organising role and document his direct assist against Serbia and Montenegro: [Messi's World Cup assists](https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/articles/lionel-messi-assists-record), [Riquelme and Messi in 2006](https://www.fifa.com/es/tournaments/mens/worldcup/articles/riquelme-messi-seleccion-argentina).

## First-pass editorial conclusion

This sprint clearly improved the 16 accepted cards: no old version won a blind comparison, 13 replacements won unanimously, and the UI review found no fit or routing regression.
