Do not consult the editorial record while scoring this sheet. For each pair, choose **A**, **B** or **tie**. A revision should win only when it is clearly better; uncertainty should be scored as a tie. Then mark whether the winner feels more:

- specific to this World Cup edition;
- useful to someone watching the player;

## Targeted correction-pass blind review

The later rendered review reopened only Puskás 1954, Zidane 1998, Xavi 2010 and Cruyff 1974. Two fresh reviewers received randomized previous-versus-correction pairs for the four generic portraits and the three attached Best XI/HM rationales. They did not receive sources, classifications or an answer key.

|---|---|---|---|

| Zidane 1998 generic | B — correction | A — correction | Correction accepted unanimously |
| Xavi 2010 generic | A — correction | B — correction | Correction accepted unanimously |
| Puskás 1954 HM | B — correction | A — correction | Correction accepted unanimously |
| Zidane 1998 Best XI | A — correction | B — correction | Correction accepted unanimously |
| Xavi 2010 HM | B — correction | A — correction | Correction accepted unanimously |
| Cruyff 1974 first correction | A — previous | A — correction | Split; correction rejected |

Cruyff's rejected correction was:

> Cruyff organises the attack by moving away from a fixed centre-forward position. He moves toward the ball to create an extra option, then joins the Netherlands' collective press after possession is lost.

It removed the unsupported pressing curve but became too general for one reviewer. A narrower second correction restored a supported positional sequence:

> Cruyff organises the Netherlands by roaming away from a fixed centre-forward position. He moves toward the ball to create an extra option, then leaves that space for a teammate as the positions rotate. When possession is lost, he joins the swarm around the ball.

Two additional fresh reviewers saw only the previous card and this revised correction in opposite A/B orders. Both selected the revised correction. It therefore became the final Cruyff card.

All seven correction-pass finals survived their decisive blind comparison. No accepted card from outside the four-portrait correction scope was included.
